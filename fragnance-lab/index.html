Ingredient chemistry, extraction methods, and safety notes are compiled from general perfumery knowledge, IFRA-style safety guidance, and common cosmetic allergen references.

Real perfume notes, concentrations, and prices reflect general fragrance-community consensus and public retail listings — they're a fair approximation, not exact figures, and can vary by batch, region, and retailer.

Regional Focus

Indian perfume & attar guide

Attars, oud, and the budget mass-market brands that dominate Indian fragrance shelves.

What's an attar?

An attar (or ittar) is a traditional oil-based fragrance, historically distilled directly into a base like sandalwood oil rather than diluted in alcohol. Alcohol-free by tradition, attars are worn undiluted on skin and tend to be extremely long-lasting.

Why oud dominates gifting culture

Oud (agarwood resin) is prized across South Asia and the Gulf for its smoky, animalic depth. Concentrated oud oils and oud-amber blends are a default gifting and festive-occasion category in India, independent of Western niche pricing.

The budget body-spray market

Brands like Fogg, Wild Stone, Engage, and Park Avenue built India's largest fragrance category: sub-₹300 deodorant body sprays sold in every kirana store, distinct from the EDP/EDT market above them.

Neither is inherently "better" — they solve different problems in a formula.

ConsistencyVaries by harvest, climate, regionIdentical every batch

ComplexityHundreds of trace compounds, hard to fully replicateSingle, precise molecule

Vegan vs. Cruelty-Free

Ethics checker

Pick a perfume to see an ingredient-by-ingredient vegan breakdown, or browse everything animal-derived across the catalogue.

Vegan means no ingredient is derived from an animal (musk deer, civet, whale ambergris, beeswax, etc.) — that's what this catalogue tracks, ingredient by ingredient. Cruelty-free is a separate claim about whether the finished product or its ingredients were tested on animals, which depends on the brand's testing policy and region, not on formula composition. A perfume can be vegan but not independently verified cruelty-free, or vice versa. This site only has the data to assess the former.
